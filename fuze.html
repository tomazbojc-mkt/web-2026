Frequently Asked Questions

Gamification & Player Engagement Software

What problems does Fuze™ solve for operators?

Fuze™ solves high acquisition costs, bonus abuse, and player churn. By reacting instantly to how players play, Fuze™ turns raw gameplay into a dynamic, interactive experience that provides reasons to return, progress, and engage more deeply, directly maximizing Lifetime Value (LTV).

Which games and verticals is Fuze™ compatible with?

Fuze™ is entirely platform and provider agnostic. It works seamlessly across Bragg proprietary games, third-party slots, aggregated games, and your sportsbook platform. It eliminates vertical silos.

Can Fuze™ be customized to match our brand and segmentation strategy?

Yes. Fuze™ is fully configurable and brand-customisable. Operators can tailor:

  • Campaign mechanics, triggers, and rewards
  • Player segmentation rules (VIPs, casuals, new players, reactivation cohorts, etc.)
  • Visual elements and UX to align with brand identity

Campaigns can be adjusted in real time, allowing teams to test, optimise, and localise engagement strategies per market or player segment.

Is Fuze™ compliant with regulated gaming markets?

Yes. Fuze™ is built to operate within regulated iGaming environments. It supports market-specific compliance requirements, responsible gaming tools, player protections, and configurable controls to ensure promotions meet local regulatory standards.

How do I measure campaign performance using Fuze™?

Fuze™ provides real-time reporting and analytics dashboards that allow operators to track campaign performance across key KPIs, including:

  • Player participation and engagement rates
  • Retention and reactivation performance
  • Bonus cost and ROI
  • Uplift in play frequency and spend

These insights allow operators to continuously optimize campaigns, adjust mechanics on the fly, and clearly demonstrate commercial impact.
